Alexane Vitte is an image maker & creative director based in Paris. Since 2023 she has been image & communication assistant at Coperni, working on campaigns, shows and brand content. She trained in design at École Duperré and École Boulle, completed a Master 2 in Design with a residency at the Mobilier National, then a Master in Fashion Image at the Institut Français de la Mode. For access to the full portfolio, please send an email to alexane.vitte@gmail.com.
